What you do in here, every module

The same four steps as your shop class, in the same order, so nothing new to figure out.

  1. 1

    Read the module

    Information sheets with the diagrams from the CBLM, split into short screens. Saved on your phone once you open it.

  2. 2

    Do the job sheet

    Step-by-step work with the tools on your bench. Tick off each step as you finish it, even with the wifi off.

  3. 3

    Take the quiz

    Ten questions from the self-check. You see which items you missed and can retake it twice.

  4. 4

    Get signed off

    Your instructor checks your output against the performance criteria and marks the competency achieved.

This is it on your phone

Same screens your instructor sees on the laptop, laid out for a 5-inch phone held one-handed in the shop.

The competencies you'll finish

Four core units of the EPAS NC II qualification, plus the common units your class already started.

TESDA training regulation

ELC724335

Assemble electronic products

Prepare the unit and workplace, mount and solder components on a PCB, then test the assembled board.

6 modules18 job sheets
ELC724331

Prepare and interpret technical drawings

Read schematic symbols, block diagrams and wiring layouts, and sketch a circuit from a written spec.

4 modules9 job sheets
ELC724333

Use hand tools and test instruments

Set the multimeter dial for voltage, resistance and continuity, and take a reading without damaging the meter.

3 modules7 job sheets
ELC724334

Terminate and connect wiring and circuits

Strip, splice and terminate conductors, and check your joints for cold solder and shorts before power-up.

5 modules12 job sheets
